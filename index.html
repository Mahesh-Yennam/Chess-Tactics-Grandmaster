♟️ How to Play & Master Tactics

1. Game Modes

  • Tactics Puzzles: Solve tactical scenarios curated from real master games (Mate-in-1, Mate-in-2, Forks, Pins, Skewers, Discovered Attacks). Gain rating points for every solved puzzle!
  • Play vs AI: Challenge the built-in chess engine with customizable difficulty levels (Novice, Club, Master).
  • 2-Player Pass & Play: Play face-to-face with a friend on the same screen.

2. Controls & Interaction

  • Drag & Drop: Drag any of your pieces and drop it onto a highlighted destination square.
  • Click-to-Move: Click a piece to view valid legal moves, then click the target square to execute the move.
  • Special Rules: Castling, En Passant, and Pawn Promotion are fully automated and validated.

3. Shortcuts

  • H: Show Tactical Hint
  • F: Flip Board perspective
  • Ctrl+Z: Take back last move
  • N: Next Puzzle / New Match
